Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I confirm product availability?

Call, email or use the form once it is active. Stock changes, so we confirm availability before you visit.

Do you sell new and pre-owned camera gear?

Yes. Available models and the condition of pre-owned items can change. Final details are confirmed in store.

Can you help check camera and lens compatibility?

Yes. Include your camera model and lens mount in the description so our team can help you compare compatible options.

Can I reserve or buy an item online?

No. This website is informational. Product confirmation and purchases are completed in person at the shop.

Do you provide cleaning or repair support?

Contact us with the equipment model and problem. Available service options and costs are confirmed after inspection.

What should I include in my message?

Include the product or service you need, your equipment model, and enough detail for us to understand your question. Do not send passwords or payment details.
