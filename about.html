A local home for camera creators.

Pteas Camera

Understand the gear before you choose it.

Pteas Camera is an information-led camera shop near Pub Street in Siem Reap. We help customers explore cameras, lenses, lighting, audio, drones and the accessories that connect a complete setup.

Our website is here to introduce the categories and services available through the store. Purchases happen in person, where equipment can be discussed and current availability can be confirmed.

01 / WHO WE HELP

For first steps and bigger ideas.

You do not need to know every technical term before visiting. Start with what you want to make.

EVERYDAY

Everyday Customers

People looking for a first camera, family photography gear, travel equipment or a practical upgrade.

CREATORS

Content Creators

Vloggers, streamers and social-media creators building a reliable camera, sound and lighting setup.

PRODUCTION

Photo & Video Makers

Photographers and filmmakers comparing equipment, expanding a kit or maintaining working gear.

02 / HOW WE WORK

Simple, practical and in person.

Our goal is to make the next step clearer, whether that means choosing, setting up or servicing equipment.

01
