01 / OUR STORY

A clearer way to compare camera gear.

Pteas Camera is an information-led camera and creator-gear shop near Pub Street in Siem Reap. We bring cameras, lenses, lighting, audio, drones and accessories together so customers can compare a complete setup in person.

Use this catalogue as a starting point, then confirm current stock, final price, listed condition and included items with the shop before making a decision.

Support that starts with your idea.

Every setup begins with a purpose. Whether you are learning photography, building a video kit or preparing for travel, we help you compare equipment around the way you plan to use it.
