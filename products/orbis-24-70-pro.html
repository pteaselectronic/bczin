Product overview

One lens for changing assignments.

The Orbis 24-70mm F2.8 Pro covers wide environmental views, natural everyday framing and short-telephoto portraits without requiring a lens change. Its F2.8 aperture remains available throughout the zoom range, supporting low-light work and controlled background separation.

A quick internal focus system keeps operation responsive while the sealed barrel adds reassurance during demanding location shoots. Confirm the available lens mount with the store before visiting so compatibility with your camera body can be checked.
