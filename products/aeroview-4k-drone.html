Full description

A compact platform for new viewpoints

The AeroView 4K Foldable Drone is designed for photographers and filmmakers who want an aerial perspective without carrying a large aircraft. Its folding body is easy to pack, while the stabilized camera supports smooth landscape, property, travel and location footage.

Assisted flight functions can simplify basic operation, but safe piloting, appropriate weather and local permission remain essential. The drone is listed in new condition; expected flight time depends on wind, temperature, payload and battery health, and current package contents should be confirmed with the store.
