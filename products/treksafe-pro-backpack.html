Full description

Comfortable storage for a working camera kit

The TrekSafe Pro Camera Backpack 28L balances camera protection with the flexibility needed for a full day away from the studio. Its configurable interior can separate a body and multiple lenses, while accessory pockets keep batteries, cards and cables from moving around inside the bag.

A padded laptop compartment and supportive carrying system make it suitable for commuting, travel and location photography. This example is listed in used condition; visible condition and included dividers should be confirmed in store before planning a kit around it.
