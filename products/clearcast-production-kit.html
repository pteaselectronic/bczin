Full description

A useful foundation for a mobile video setup

The ClearCast Production Kit brings three frequently used creator tools together in one coordinated package. The field monitor gives camera operators a larger view of the image, while the directional microphone helps isolate dialogue and the wireless link allows a director or assistant to monitor from a more convenient position.

Its compact components suit interviews, short films, events and social-content production where portability matters. The kit is listed in new condition and is intended as an accessible starting point for expanding a mirrorless or cinema-camera rig.
