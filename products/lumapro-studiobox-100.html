Product overview

Shape soft light with simple controls.

The LumaPro StudioBox 100 pairs a 100-watt LED source with an included rectangular softbox to create broad, gentle illumination. Adjustable color temperature helps the light match warmer indoor fixtures or cooler daylight, reducing the time needed for correction during shooting.

The stand and modifier make it a practical starting point for portraits, interviews, online presentations and tabletop work. Its quiet active cooling is designed for sets where dialogue is being captured, while the compact kit remains manageable for small rooms and mobile creators.
