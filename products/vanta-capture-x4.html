Product overview

Flexible performance for hybrid creators.

The Vanta Capture X4 combines a 33-megapixel full-frame sensor with quick subject detection and in-body stabilization. Its balanced controls make it approachable for photographers moving into full-frame work while preserving the manual control experienced creators expect.

For video, the camera records detailed 4K footage and uses its stabilization system to reduce small movements during handheld shooting. This pre-owned unit should be inspected in store so you can review its cosmetic condition, shutter history and included accessories before deciding whether it suits your kit.
