Pteas Camera Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through the contact form

LAST UPDATED 13 OCTOBER 2026

Pteas Camera respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the personal information that you choose to provide through the contact form on our informational website.

Current form status  The demo contact form does not send messages to Pteas Camera or store form entries. If form submission is activated later, the collection and use described in this policy will apply.

1 Form Information We Collect

The contact form is the only place where Pteas Camera asks you to provide personal information through this website. The form includes:

  • Your first name and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number if you choose to provide it
  • The subject of your inquiry
  • Your detailed description

Please do not include payment-card details, passwords, identity documents, medical information or other sensitive information in your description.

Information We Do Not Request

Pteas Camera does not request account passwords, online-payment details, job applications, CVs, precise device location or social-media login information through this website.

2 How We Use Form Information

If the contact form is activated, we will use the information you submit only to:

  • Reply to your question
  • Discuss product availability, new or pre-owned gear and compatibility
  • Answer questions about repairs, cleaning or creator setups
  • Help you prepare for a visit to the store
  • Protect the form and respond to misuse when necessary

Submitting the form does not create an order, reservation, sale or service contract.

3 When We Share Information

We do not sell, rent or trade form information. We may share it only with a service provider that helps deliver or host the form, when necessary to respond to your request, to comply with applicable law, or to protect the rights and safety of Pteas Camera and others.

Any service provider handling form information should use it only for the service it provides to Pteas Camera and apply reasonable safeguards.

4 Storage and Retention

The current demo form does not send entries to a server or database controlled by Pteas Camera. If the form is activated, its privacy notice and this policy will be updated to identify how submissions are delivered and stored.

We will keep submitted information only for as long as reasonably needed to answer and follow up on your request, resolve a dispute or meet an applicable legal requirement. Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or securely disposed of.

5 Data Security

When form collection is activated, Pteas Camera will use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect submitted information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

6 Your Choices

You may ask us to explain what form information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information or delete information that is no longer needed. We may ask for enough information to verify that the request relates to you.

You may also choose not to use the form and contact the store by phone, email or in person.

7 Cookies and Third Party Services

Pteas Camera does not set first-party cookies and does not use website analytics or advertising trackers. We do not use cookies to build visitor profiles or send targeted advertising.

This website embeds Google Maps, loads a web font and links to social-media platforms. When third-party content loads, its provider may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details, and may use its own cookies under its privacy policy. That processing is controlled by the third party, not by Pteas Camera.

Opening an external link takes you away from this website. Review the provider's privacy information before sharing personal information with it.

8 Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, contact form or our information practices change. The updated date at the start of this document will show when the latest version took effect.

For a privacy question or request, call +855 (0) 123 456 789, email pteascamera@gmail.com, or visit Pteas Camera at the location shown on the Contact page.
